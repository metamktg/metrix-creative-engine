anonymous template. Use the guarded submit path; the full reference stays on demand.

These rules supplement the render bridge and production review. They do not grant spend/publication permission or replace current user instructions.

Before spending, lock the actual advertiser, audience, supported promise, exact hook/body/CTA, destination/conditions, duration mode, accepted references and acoustic profile. Keep casting, geography, client exclusions, chosen voice/music and numerical settings in the private run profile. Preserve earlier authorization and component acceptance. Keep one canonical `script.json`: whole approved monologue, permitted wording changes, hook-treatment rationale, source/claim links and line-level thought, intention, emotion and observable behavior. Bind actual cast/voice/scene fit, complete accepted voice and current provider capability evidence. Requested emotion is direction; observed delivery and acceptance require the actual media. A new presenter invalidates performer approval while suitable B-roll/UI originals retain their source/rights acceptance.

Before dependent lip sync, run offline `kie_validate_ugc_preproduction` (CLI `ugc-check`) and submit through `kie_create_ugc_lipsync` (CLI `ugc-start`). These paths validate current file/request bindings before creating a task; raw task tools remain general generation primitives and do not provide this guard. Do not bypass the guarded creator route to evade missing evidence. The approved-full-voice guard does not accept native-audio production; that architecture needs its own explicitly qualified contract. A structural pass cannot hear a voice, certify acting, reserve provider funds atomically or replace the five final reviews.

Target a composed, conversational person with natural thought groups and selective emphasis. Reject announcer projection, forced hook lift, identical clause attacks, unlimited-breath delivery, clipped tails, fake breaths/imperfections and inconsistent room/mic sound. Keep speaking lips synchronized; inspect/listen before expensive dependent work under the actual review agreement.

For a creator opening, choose one primary text message and preserve face clearance. Avoid a large headline plate stacked with competing full captions. Measure readable caption fit at phone size. Give B-roll a relevant action and usable native range. Keep creator/UI continuity; use authentic captures, purposeful overlays and selected readable full-screen proof. Reject static posing, awkward gaze/expression, screenshot slideshows, arbitrary cut quotas, ghosted/doubled UI, blank blips and stale closing beats. Effects need a communication purpose, not a quota.

Keep music noticeable below clear speech, with placement-specific rights and a gentle performance-aware entrance. Inspect isolated stems for duplicate voices, source dialogue, competing vocals or delay. Avoid nuisance SFX and never cover a bad voice with music. Preserve complete CTA words, a correct offer/destination and sufficient reading time.

Reuse before generation; quote, inspect and reserve before submitting. Keep budgets by unit, unknown charges held and repair loops finite. Never batch an uninspected quote with its paid dependent call. Use relevant tool metadata and concise receipts rather than full catalogs.

Model negatives belong only in supported fields. Directing notes are not spoken text. A generic negative list cannot replace actual output review. Bind reviews to the current export; distinguish measurements, ASR, frames and actual AV access. Reconcile model timing against local facts; keep optional style notes separate from blockers. Never fabricate a human/blind pass. Respect explicit review deferrals without relabeling them PASS.

Keep stable versions, sources/stems/recipes/rights and exact review limits. Apply the indexed keep-three policy (`scripts/retain_drafts.py`, `docs/draft-retention.md`) only after dependency/recovery checks, from a reviewed plan. Finish when the brief and agreed review criteria are met; do not reopen accepted work indefinitely to chase reviewer taste.

Resolve the current private client library before reusing historical media. Apply current exclusions across speech, captions, UI and CTA; a new crop does not make a shared gesture a new performance. Track native source windows and motion-base lineage. Choose sector-relevant B-roll and purposeful creator returns. Cache by complete source/overlay/audio/render identity, never frame count alone.

Use the configured feedback provider for media review and repository for storage. Verify actual review modalities and upload readbacks. An offline sync plan is not an unattended uploader.
