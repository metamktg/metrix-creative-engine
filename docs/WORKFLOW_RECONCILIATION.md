## Reusable rules and their scope

| Decision | Shared rule |
| --- | --- |
| Narration | Coherent accepted performance, consistent phrase entrances/levels/timbre, and source-aware diagnosis of apparently doubled voices. Retain intentional emphasis and natural speech. |
| Music | Deliberate rights-cleared selection, audible subordinate balance when intended, no competing lyrics, and a fade/source offset that supports the opening word. Exact gains, offsets and fade lengths remain run decisions. |
| Product proof | Match the spoken feature with actual evidence and choose a deliberate format-appropriate treatment. Real capture, genuine layers/composites, reveals, annotations and readable stills are options. No screenshot-slideshow default, motion quota or inherited layout template. |
| Transitions and performance | Inspect continuous playback for double images, ghosts, collisions and awkward gaze/action/expression handoffs. Repair locally before regenerating. |
| Presenter changes | Reaccept identity and vocal performance while retaining suitable approved B-roll/UI/library originals. Old face/lip/voice approval does not transfer. |
| Evidence limits | A genuine generic feature illustration does not establish a complete captured interaction, live state or product result. Missing proof stays missing. |
| Review routes | Confirm actual full-media access/capabilities. Transcript, frame and service-error results cannot become listening or full-video PASS evidence. |
| Deferrals | Explicit temporary exceptions remain scoped to their gate/candidate and NOT REVIEWED, with authority and a resume condition. They do not remove the shared review contract. |
| Cleanup | Verify a recoverable archive/manifest, critical-source hashes, editable relinking and capacity before authorized draft removal. Preserve originals, stems, rights, ledgers and evidence. |

Declare each choice as engine-wide, brand/project or run-specific and bind an accepted reference when it carries a treatment decision. Client identity, scripts, offers, casting, exact music and project media belong in private briefs/libraries, not this shared standard.

## Validation boundaries

The local review validators check contained paths, records, hashes, selected production context, identities and required attestations. Technical measurements, decoder checks and transition detectors supplement actual listening and normal-speed review. They cannot establish naturalness, audience comprehension or a person's perception by themselves. Each final export has a current exact-file binding; edited context makes affected verdicts stale.

The assembly renderer's gain, ducking and loudness defaults remain implementation settings. Select the approved delivery profile and inspect the encoded output. Model ratio, duration, voice and price facts must be checked against the current selected provider operation; dated routing documents are not a live catalog.
