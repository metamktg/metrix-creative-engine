# Evergreen UGC/video production and review standard

This repository standard is composed from the UGC skill references. It governs production and presentation eligibility, not provider permissions or spending authority. Python 3.10 or later runs the local evidence helpers. Edit the source references, then rebuild this document with `python skills/ugc-creator-edit/scripts/build_standard_doc.py --out docs/UGC_PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md`.


## Evergreen production standard

Use this reference with the approved brief, current asset map and the parent UGC workflow. It governs production choices before the five presentation gates. It does not authorize additional deliverables, spend or publication.

The [preproduction director](../skills/ugc-creator-edit/references/preproduction-director.md) connects the existing [script-and-delivery.md](../skills/ugc-creator-edit/references/script-and-delivery.md), [casting.md](../skills/ugc-creator-edit/references/casting.md), [hooks.md](../skills/ugc-creator-edit/references/hooks.md) and [lip-sync-routing.md](../skills/ugc-creator-edit/references/lip-sync-routing.md) owners before new creator performance. Keep one canonical approved monologue, exact locks, reasoned hook treatment, credible cast/voice/scene fit, thought-level intention/emotion/behavior, truthful proof sources and current provider handoff. New performers need fresh acceptance; unchanged appropriate B-roll/UI sources retain their rights and reuse scope. Dated provider recipes are not live capabilities or universal creative policy.

Approved-full-voice Kie lip sync uses a current versioned packet, offline `kie_validate_ugc_preproduction` / client `ugc-check` and guarded `kie_create_ugc_lipsync` / client `ugc-start`. Structural validation cannot hear the full voice, establish acting quality, prove remote uploads or certify release. Native-audio routes need separate qualification and cannot borrow a standalone voice pass. Continue to apply the existing five exact-export reviews.

### Plan the sound

Record a music decision for every edit. Select a suitable instrumental bed when it supports the message and creator's delivery. Identify the exact source file and hash, rights evidence, permitted commercial and paid-media uses, restrictions, cue ranges, fades and ducking. Unknown rights block use. A generation result, platform availability or familiar sound is not proof of a commercial license.

An intentional dry treatment requires a specific creative reason and documented reviewer acceptance. Do not silently omit music, add an unlicensed bed, or use music to conceal defective speech. Retain separate voice, music, ambience and effects stems and the final mix recipe.

Let dialogue lead the mix. Duck or arrange the bed so complete words, offer conditions and the destination remain intelligible; avoid pumping, abrupt entrances and clipped endings. Check the final encoded mix on phone speakers, headphones and mono at ordinary and low listening levels. Measure against the approved delivery profile; do not substitute a universal loudness target or fixed music-to-voice ratio for listening evidence.

When music is intended to be audible, verify that it actually registers at ordinary and low playback levels while the voice stays clear. Do not equate a nonzero music stem or a good integrated-loudness reading with an audible, balanced bed. Choose an instrumental passage unless vocals serve an explicit brief purpose; inspect the selected passage for sung words that could compete with the narrator. Give the entrance a deliberate fade or a different source offset so a beat or transient does not collide with the first spoken word. Set the fade, offset, ducking and level from the actual passage and performance, not a universal gain change or fixed fade duration.

Build a coherent narration performance. Where separately generated pickups sound detached, prefer a continuous accepted read or repair only the affected phrases while preserving the approved voice. Compare phrase entrances, emphasis, pitch peaks, timbre and loudness before the final mix; normalize or ride phrases only as needed, keeping expressive intent. Do not flatten intended emphasis or accelerate speech to hide a mismatch. If the result sounds like two voices, listen to isolated stems and inspect timeline overlap, retained source audio, doubled pickups, vocal music and room/delay effects. One encoded audio stream can contain several competing voices, and final mix normalization cannot diagnose that. Record the cause, affected ranges and the narrow repair.

Define a credible recording and microphone perspective. Keep the same acoustic profile through off-camera footage, product material, graphics and the CTA. A picture change does not create a new recording event. Establish a visible speaking-setting change before changing its profile. Preserve word tails, compatible level and natural room decay. Avoid artificial hiss, breaths, damage or reflections added solely to manufacture realism.

### Let the message determine the duration

Write a concise thought-led performance with one intended action. The brief's maximum duration is a ceiling, not a slot to fill. Remove repeated setup and redundant explanations before extending the edit or accelerating speech. Preserve meaningful emphasis, natural variation, complete words and a comfortable close.

Speech speed-up is not the default pacing tool. First use useful shot lengths, silence trims that preserve articulation, clear reveals, reframing and relevant cutaways. Keep visible speech and its audio synchronized. Record source and output ranges, speed factor and processing method for every retimed span. Review the result at normal playback speed; a technically valid time map does not prove natural delivery.

Retime silent footage or navigation only when gestures, motion and the demonstrated process remain credible and truthful. Settle into a readable hold for evidence. Do not imply a faster process, omit a necessary step or conceal a defect through speed changes. Export for normal playback and inspect that export rather than relying on an accelerated review preview.

### Give each cut a purpose

Every visual change should help the viewer recognize the situation, understand a thought, inspect evidence, resolve uncertainty or act. Use direct cuts, audio bridges, match cuts and transitions where they serve that purpose. Do not impose a cut-frequency quota, synchronize every sentence to music, or add an effect merely to make the edit appear more produced.

Check entry and exit frames, word tails, visual continuity and caption changes at each cut. Give useful details time to register. Keep captions in complete thought groups, within the placement's safe regions and clear of faces and evidence. Avoid orphan words, duplicate competing overlays and abrupt changes of text hierarchy.

Choose a deliberate, format-appropriate proof treatment from the brief and any bound accepted reference. Real interaction capture, genuine UI layers/composites, restrained motion, reveals, annotations and readable static proof are available methods, not a mandatory template. Do not turn a product demonstration into an automatic screenshot slideshow, impose a motion quota or generalize one project's preferred layout into an engine default. Time a crop, reveal, highlight or hold to the corresponding spoken feature/action, with enough reading time and no disconnected static beat. A local repair should preserve unrelated accepted treatment decisions.

At any UI/composite handoff, inspect the actual transition frames for two offset copies, double images, ghosts, crowding and caption collisions. Choose a purposeful clean transition when a dissolve produces a doubled screen. Test both ends and continuous playback; a settled contact sheet can miss the defect. Do not fabricate interactive controls, clicks, answers or product states in the animation.

Match gaze, expression and body action to the spoken thought. Check the transition from addressing camera to working, not only the first and last pose. Trim an awkward look, use a clean action range or a relevant cutaway before paying for regeneration. Do not introduce a fixed glance duration or a universal smile requirement. Any reaction, gesture or angle change must feel plausible at the original normal speech rate.

### Accept B-roll before it reaches the timeline

Choose footage for relevance and believable usable action before resolution or model prestige. For each selected range, record its purpose, source hash, rights, trim handles and acceptance evidence. Check:

- Native detail after the actual delivery crop, with adequate focus, exposure and legibility.
- Credible motion cadence, stable objects and backgrounds, and no distracting looping, morphing or interpolation.
- Anatomical plausibility, hands, grips, props and any text that the viewer can inspect.
- Required subject, product, wardrobe, lighting and setting continuity.
- Consistency with the spoken meaning and supported claims, without implying an unverified result or personal experience.

Inspect the selected range and the actual encoded output at normal speed. Examine critical motion and cut frames closely; a good still is not motion acceptance. Use higher-quality existing ranges when they meet the purpose. Upscaling can improve presentation detail but cannot repair anatomy, distorted lettering, temporal drift, continuity or relevance.

When a new presenter is requested, retain suitable approved B-roll, product captures, graphics and editable sources byte for byte as library originals. Give any new crop or retimed derivative its own path and hash. Separate reusable non-identifying footage from footage containing the prior presenter's face, speaking mouth, distinctive wardrobe or voice. Inspect the exact proposed ranges for continuity; do not silently reuse the prior identity or its voice/listening approval for the new presenter. A new identity/voice needs its own acceptance while unrelated accepted product material remains available.

Preserve what a source actually proves. A genuine feature illustration can show the interface vocabulary without proving a complete interaction, a live state, a result or the answer to a question. Bind the narration to the captured evidence. If the brief requires a full product flow and only generic cards exist, record the missing capture rather than promoting those cards into proof or generating a substitute interface.

### Finish with one clear action

Carry a consistent intended action, accurate destination and necessary offer conditions into the ending. Establish the CTA while it is being spoken when that improves comprehension. Preserve its proportions and typography; measure text fit instead of stretching it. Keep the card or equivalent action treatment stable long enough to read, preserve the complete final word and recording tail, and inspect the final frame. Avoid competing closing treatments that obscure which action matters.

Editing choices are hypotheses about attention, comprehension and response. Do not label them proven CRO, hook-rate, retention or conversion improvements without applicable delivery evidence.

### Execution and acceptance records

Extend the shot plan with these columns:

`Timecode | Viewer question/purpose | Visual/action/framing | Source hash/ranges | Output ranges/speed | On-camera status/acoustic profile | Transition/GFX | Music/ambience/SFX cue and rights | Acceptance evidence`

Retain the music decision and rights evidence, stems and mix recipe; source/crop and speed maps; selected B-roll acceptance evidence; current transcript and captions; actual-export technical measurements; normal-speed playback and listening records; and a concise timecoded defect/review log. Bind reviews to the exact current export and relevant sources. A later edit invalidates affected acceptance and requires the appropriate re-review.

Record the brief-specific editing criteria with timecode, status and evidence: narration coherence/phrase consistency, competing-voice diagnosis when applicable, music audibility and entrance, matching product/spoken beats, UI/composite continuity, gaze/action continuity and readable proof/CTA holds. Mark each decision's scope as engine-wide, brand/project or this run, and bind accepted style references by path/hash where used. A project preference does not become a shared default. These are review criteria within the bound profile and editorial evidence, not invented provider parameters or a claim that an automatic detector judged them.

### Preserve a recoverable production state

Before clearing superseded drafts, distinguish accepted library originals, editable projects, linked media, stems, rights, job/cost ledgers, review evidence and disposable render intermediates. Build a recovery manifest of the material to preserve with contained paths, file sizes and hashes for retained critical sources. Verify the destination copy/archive can be read and that the editable recipe can resolve its media. Check working and archive free space before creating another full package or render. A failed or partial archive is not authorization to remove its source.

Use the user's authorized cleanup scope and retain an explicit restore/resume entry point. Moving recoverable drafts out of the active folder is different from permanently deleting sources. Never delete accepted originals, unrelated user work, dependencies or inaccessible paths merely because disk space is low. State any unreadable or unverified archive ranges honestly. Provider job recovery alone is not a production archive.

Use these artifacts at the mandatory gates:

| Gate ID | Production evidence it needs |
| --- | --- |
| `iap_variables` | Brief and variable fidelity, technical delivery profile, source/crop/speed maps, sync, text fit, rights and actual-export measurements. |
| `communications` | Clear opening, concise supported message, useful evidence, coherent sequence, one intended action and accurate conditions. |
| `sound_check` | Actual final audio comprehension, intelligible complete words and destination, plain-language understanding without production explanation, and accessible captions. |
| `editorial_peer` | Actual encoded playback, listening and close inspection of cuts, B-roll, mix, captions and ending; specific defects resolved against the current files. |
| `blind_review` | The current neutral review copy and necessary de-identified context, without prior scores or the producer's rationale; an objective assessment of comprehension, distraction and action. |

These are evidence requirements, not automatic passes. Record the actual reviewer, method and limits. Automated measurements, frame inspection and another agent's assessment do not establish human listening or playback acceptance. Missing rights or required review evidence remains unresolved; do not fabricate approval to present an asset as ready.

## Five mandatory presentation gates

Apply this contract to every finished creator-led UGC/video deliverable, including new versions and placement exports. All five gates must be PASS before approved client presentation. Generation success, a technical pass, a polished frame or a senior-sounding summary is not a substitute.

This is a local orchestration standard. It does not add provider API fields, spend authority or a server-enforced publication block.

### Prepare one current candidate

Finish the authorized edit, then create a local evidence bundle. Retain the exact master, approved brief, current spoken transcript, complete mixed voice, captions, edit decisions, source/rights map, delivery profile, approved registry snapshot, neutral blind packet and exact blind review copy. Prepare that packet/copy before reviews; the blind reviewer receives them only at the final gate.

Use relative local paths inside the run. The registry is an approved JSON snapshot with `approved_codes`, pinned to its authoritative source in the source map. Do not turn a regex extraction, code template or remembered prefix into an allowed-code list. IAP identifiers and technical media variables are separate.

The profile declares expected format/timing, placement/safe regions, sound/delivery requirements, and `sound_check_required_facts` for message, benefit, action and essential conditions. These facts are declared before the lay review. Where no material condition exists, state that explicitly instead of inventing one.

New creator runs set `creator_preproduction_required: true` in that profile and
bind `production.preproduction` to one `{path, sha256}` descriptor or a nonempty
array listing every actual operation packet directly. A manifest proxy does not
bind indirectly referenced packet contents.
Before final review rerun the offline Node preflight defined in
[preproduction-director.md](../skills/ugc-creator-edit/references/preproduction-director.md). The final Python
validator rehashes the packet and includes its descriptor in review context;
it does not rerun preflight or recursively inspect nested files. Missing a
required packet is NOT_CHECKED, and a changed packet invalidates the bound
review context. Existing artifact roles and all five gates remain unchanged.

Include the brief-specific production criteria from `production-standard.md` in the profile and evidence: coherent narration/phrase consistency, audible subordinate music and its entrance, matching spoken/product beats, format-appropriate proof treatment, clean image handoffs, gaze/action continuity and readable holds. Bind any accepted treatment reference and its decision scope rather than imposing an inherited layout. No single numeric loudness threshold or final mix normalization certifies these judgments. Each final placement or captioned/clean export needs its own versioned record bound to its exact master; shared component evidence needs an explicit current-export binding.

Copy `skills/ugc-creator-edit/templates/review-manifest.json` into the run, replace its paths and deliberately fill the production decisions. Use the preparation helper to hash the files and create five PENDING gates:

```text
python <skill>/scripts/prepare_review_record.py <run>/review-manifest.json --root <run> --out production-review.json
python <skill>/scripts/validate_review_gates.py <run>/production-review.json --root <run>
```

The helper never creates a review, marks a gate PASS or overwrites an existing review record. A new candidate needs a new version. Keep approval and rework history.

### Mandatory reviews

| Gate | Required review |
| --- | --- |
| `iap_variables` | Check actual approved registry membership and brief variables; matrix isolation/locks where applicable; actual encoded format, duration, frame rate, dimensions, ratio, codec, colour, audio, word/caption/CTA fidelity, safe regions, source provenance, rights, source ranges and speed/sync map. Compare each technical value with the bound brief/profile and keep measurement evidence. |
| `communications` | Judge the actual spoken and visible message: relevant hook and payoff, supported promise, understandable mechanism or evidence, concise progression, one intended action, accurate destination and essential conditions. No invented results, testimony, urgency or prelaunch CRO/retention improvement. |
| `sound_check` | The “12-Year-Old Sound Check”: an independent adult lay listener, or a declared audio-capable reviewer, hears the complete export once at 1x before reading the script. Capture unprompted answers: what is this, why does it matter, what should I do, and what conditions matter? Compare with the predeclared facts, then check captions and meaning muted at phone size. Do not recruit minors or equate one review with universal accessibility. |
| `editorial_peer` | A colleague who did not produce or edit the candidate watches and hears the full actual export at 1x, reviews the phone-size muted version, checks every cut, B-roll, transitions, mix, captions, CTA and final word/tail, and closes specific timecoded defects. Identify human and agent reviewers accurately. |
| `blind_review` | A separate fresh reviewer makes the final assessment from a neutral packet and the actual current review copy. Withhold authors, provider/model, cost, versions and prior verdicts. Use the same fixed clarity/delivery/proof/CTA rubric, lock the verdict after the other four gates complete and before unblinding, and report residual identity cues honestly. |

No weighted average can hide a failed mandatory check. Mark each required check as passed, failed or unperformed with evidence. The checks in `GATE_CHECKS` in the validator are the machine-readable checklist names.

#### Listening and independence

ASR equality verifies recognized words; waveform measurements verify signal properties. Neither proves vocal naturalness, articulation, comprehension or human listening. An agent that only received text or frames cannot submit `full_audio_1x` or `full_av_1x` evidence.

Record reviewer kind as `human`, `audio_capable_agent`, `agent` or `automated`, together with actual capability and method. The listening, editorial and blind gates need a human or declared audio-capable agent with real full-media access. Do not rename an automated or text-only check to satisfy them.

Verify a review route's actual input handling before depending on it: confirm it receives the intended encoded video and audio rather than only an attachment name, transcript, sparse frames or a failed upload. Retain the tool receipt, access/method limits and any sampling gaps. Provider availability or an eloquent answer is not proof of media access. Do not convert a service error, partial review or capability probe into a full-media PASS.

The sound-check listener is a dedicated first-exposure reviewer, distinct from production/editing, all other gate reviewers, and applicability/music reviewers recorded in the context. Editorial review cannot be self-review. Blind review uses another reviewer, distinct from contributors, every prior gate reviewer and the recorded applicability/music reviewers, with no inherited project conversation or earlier feedback. Use stable opaque IDs; identity comparisons ignore outer whitespace and letter case, while the original context remains hash-bound.

#### Honest blind review and anonymity

Use a randomized neutral asset ID and a minimal de-identified objective/audience/offer packet. Keep the private mapping from its hashed review copy to the exact master. Do not include source paths, account details, creator reputation, model labels, price or earlier grades in the packet.

A fresh context means a new reviewer session with only that packet and media. Passing a same-history subagent the instruction “be blind” is not blinding. Lock the verdict before revealing the production history.

This contract records `process_blind`, not guaranteed identity blindness. Logos, domains, voices or category cues can remain in legitimate creative. Record the residual cues; stripping a filename is insufficient. If identity masking is needed, make a reviewed derivative, hash it separately and disclose which changes could affect judgment. Do not silently remove necessary offer facts or pretend the derivative is the exact delivery master.

Reusable procedures, templates, fixtures, shared learning and public summaries contain no client, brand, industry or niche case details. Private run evidence retains only the identity/context necessary to produce and judge that actual creative.

### Evidence contract

`production-review.json` has `schema_version: 1`, a neutral asset ID, `artifacts`, `contributors`, `iap`, `production` and exactly five `gates`.

- Required artifact roles: `master`, `brief`, `script`, `voice`, `captions`, `edl`, `sources`, `profile`, `registry`, `blind_packet`, `blind_copy`. Each has a contained relative path and SHA256.
- `contributors` declares actual producer/editor IDs. `iap` declares standard or matrix mode, exact registry codes, applicability decision/reason/reviewer and matrix-lock evidence where applicable. A standard brief with no IAP codes needs an explicit non-applicability decision; the technical and other gates still apply.
- `production` records music selection or a specifically justified editorial dry exception, selected B-roll acceptance and a source/output speed map. Read [production-standard.md](../skills/ugc-creator-edit/references/production-standard.md). A normal-speed export is not permission to hide misleading source retiming.
- The blind packet/copy are bound artifact roles before any review. The final gate must reference these exact hashes; swapping a newly hashed derivative cannot preserve an earlier verdict.
- A PASS gate records reviewer, current master SHA256, context `bundle_sha256`, timezone-aware completion time, hashed evidence, required checks and no open blocking defects.
- Evidence methods distinguish `artifact_check`, `text_review`, `frame_inspection`, `full_audio_1x`, `full_av_1x` and `muted_phone`. Required methods must describe what actually happened.
- Sound-check evidence also retains the first-exposure, unprimed listening declaration, four unprompted answers and comparison with the pinned facts.
- Blind evidence retains the exact packet/copy hashes, exclusions, residual-cue review, freshness declaration, locked verdict and any later unblinding time.

The context digest binds artifact role hashes **and** the contributors, IAP and production decisions. It excludes gate verdicts to avoid recursion. The validator exposes `bundle_hash(record)` for preparing the value; reviewers must bind their own verdict after checking the current files and context.

All applicable technical variables must appear in the bound profile/source/measurement evidence. The validator checks local files, registry membership, record completeness, speed-map arithmetic, hashes and reviewer attestations. It does not independently probe every media property or certify what a reviewer perceived. Never describe a structured-record PASS as proof of perceptual quality.

### Release, repair and re-review

Run the validator immediately before approved presentation and retain its output:

| Exit | State | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | READY | All five evidence-bound gates pass. Present only the exact checked candidate. |
| 1 | FAIL | A rejection, unsafe path, invalid record, unresolved defect or stale/tampered binding blocks release. |
| 2 | NOT_CHECKED | Required review/evidence is missing, pending or unavailable. Complete it before release. |

When both failures and missing checks exist, FAIL takes precedence. An incomplete check is never a positive quality verdict.

Record each defect against the actual export hash and timecode. Make the smallest correction that preserves the brief, rights and comparison validity. Reuse accepted sources before paid replacement. A new master or relevant context change makes prior approvals stale; reviewers must explicitly reaffirm or repeat the appropriate work against the new exact candidate. Component evidence may remain useful, but copying old verdicts onto a new hash is not re-review.

Keep unfinished candidates internal and labeled with their pending gates. A working preview is not approved client presentation. Continue other authorized work while a gate is pending; do not invent a colleague, a blind session or a human listening decision to unblock it. Do not retroactively label earlier work as having passed this new standard.

If the user temporarily defers a check for a particular run, record the exact instruction, affected gate/candidate, limits and condition for resuming. Preserve the gate as deferred/NOT REVIEWED with its missing evidence; the validator remains non-READY. This permits authorized independent internal progress, not a synthetic PASS or an evergreen removal of that check. Do not apply one run's exception to other briefs, exports or later sessions.
