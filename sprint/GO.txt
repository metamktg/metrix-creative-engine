Run the creative sprint.

Everything you need is in this repo and in sprint/. Do not ask me for paths,
counts, or confirmations that you can work out yourself. Work autonomously
until you hit the one stop below.

INPUTS
  sprint/briefs/    the briefs file from Metrix. Render one asset per brief,
                    however many there are. The count is not a rule.
  sprint/refs/      reference images. Upload them all, use the returned URLs
                    as image_input. Without them the model invents the product.
  sprint/out/       everything you produce goes here.
  brand-kits/eastcoastartstudio/brand-kit.json
  brand-kits/eastcoastartstudio/MASTER_EXECUTION_PROMPT.md

DO THIS, IN ORDER, WITHOUT ASKING
  1. Read the brand kit. client_account_rules and conflicts_to_resolve
     override the briefs.
  2. Read MASTER_EXECUTION_PROMPT.md and follow it.
  3. Upload every file in sprint/refs/ and keep the URLs.
  4. Run kie_validate_brief on every brief.
  5. Check credits. Tell me the number and the estimated spend in one line.
  6. Render TWO cells from different rows. Download them to sprint/out/.
     Show them to me.

  --> THIS IS THE ONLY STOP. Wait for my yes.

  7. On yes: render the rest. One model for the whole batch, nano-banana-2,
     aspect_ratio "4:5" set explicitly, resolution 2K. Download every asset to
     sprint/out/ with its MST name as you go, never at the end.
  8. Report: one table, one row per brief. Cell, pass/fail on the five
     alignment rows, credits, filename. Then the total spent and anything you
     could not make pass.

RULES THAT ARE NOT NEGOTIABLE
  - The product is a painted city scene with a real legible street nameplate.
    It is NOT a map.
  - Never render "3,000+ cities".
  - The hook and the CTA go IN the image, quoted from copy_architecture.
    CTA is "SHOP NOW" on a solid black button.
  - No emoji. The phrase "Read why" is banned.
  - No invented reviews, ratings or counts. There is no social proof yet.
  - Read every character of rendered text before accepting an asset.

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
  Fix it and tell me in the report. Do not stop to ask unless it is the
  matrix isolation check failing, or you would have to spend credits on
  something I did not authorise.
